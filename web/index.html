    What this never does

    • No lookup of a person by phone number, email, or handle.
    • No contact details in public fields; the API rejects them.
    • No DMs, no cold emails, no invite blasts.
    • No unverified profiles in your results.
    How matching actually works

    Six factors, published weights: goal 30%, interests 25%, availability 15%, communication 12%, timezone 10%, momentum 8%. No shared goal caps a pair at 0.22 however well the rest lines up. Every score breaks down per factor and the engine is services/matcher.

    Your data

    GET /v1/export returns everything held about you. DELETE /v1/people/:id erases the profile and its consents. Revoking a consent deletes the signals derived from it in the same transaction.

    Tandem is a reference implementation, not a hosted service. Run it yourself: make dev. Read docs/consent-model.md before pointing it at real people.
